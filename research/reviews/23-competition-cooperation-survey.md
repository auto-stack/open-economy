主要交付为[专题survey](../surveys/03-competition-cooperation-distribution/README.md)，分别研究[不公平竞争](../surveys/03-competition-cooperation-distribution/unfair-competition.md)与[开放协作及收益分配](../surveys/03-competition-cooperation-distribution/open-cooperation-and-distribution.md)。[两个有限例子](../surveys/03-competition-cooperation-distribution/models.md)核查私人获利条件和预算平衡与合作稳定的区别；它们不是现实参数估计。
